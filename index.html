Where I can help

Sometimes you don't need more experience. You need to explain the experience you already have.

A CV has limited space. An interview has limited time. The challenge is often figuring out which parts of your experience matter for a particular role, and how to talk about them without sounding rehearsed.

Your CV isn't getting enough traction

We look at the role you're targeting and work out whether your CV is making the relevant parts of your experience easy to find.

You have an interview coming up

We break down what the company is likely trying to assess and practise how you can demonstrate it using your own experience.

You're starting out

You don't need a long employment history to have examples worth talking about. Projects, university work, internships, open source and personal projects can all be useful.

How I work

We start with the role, not with a generic checklist.

Every role is different. Rather than giving you a standard list of interview answers or CV templates, we look at the specific opportunity you're interested in.

01 — UNDERSTAND

What is the company actually looking for?

We look at the job description, the level, and the kind of problems the role is likely to involve.

02 — CONNECT

Where is that evidence in your experience?

We find concrete examples from your work, studies or projects that demonstrate the skills the role needs.

03 — COMMUNICATE

How can you explain it clearly?

We work on your CV, your examples and, when relevant, your interview answers. The aim isn't to memorise scripts.
