The Career Bridge approach

Your experience already contains the evidence.

You don't need to invent a better version of yourself. The goal is to find the strongest evidence in your experience, understand why it matters for the role, and communicate it clearly.

01 — IDENTIFY

Find what you bring.

Look beyond job titles and responsibilities to identify strengths, patterns, impact and evidence in your experience.

02 — CONNECT

Understand what matters.

Break down the role you're targeting and connect its requirements to the experience and strengths you already have.

03 — SHOW

Make it visible.

Turn those connections into clearer CV content, stronger stories and better interview answers.
