See the difference
Better wording isn't the goal.
Better evidence is.

Imagine a role asking for experience designing scalable distributed systems.

The job description

“Experience designing scalable distributed systems.”

A generic CV bullet

“Worked on distributed systems and improved system performance.”

The questions we'd ask

What were you actually responsible for? What problem were you solving? What constraints mattered? What decisions did you make? What changed as a result?

The point isn't to make the sentence sound impressive. It's to find the evidence that shows you can actually do the job.

FAQ

A few practical questions.

Do you rewrite CVs?

Not as a standalone service. We first understand the role you're targeting and then improve how your experience is presented for that role.

Do I need to have an interview scheduled?

No. Preparing before you start applying can be useful, particularly if you're unsure what your target roles are likely to assess.

Do I need professional experience?

No. University projects, internships, open-source contributions and personal projects can all provide useful evidence of how you work and what you can do.

Do you work with people outside Italy?

Yes. Sessions are online and can be conducted in English or Italian.

Do you only work with Software Engineers?

Software Engineers are the main focus of The Career Bridge. If your background is closely related to software development, feel free to get in touch and describe what you're working towards.

Why are the initial sessions offered at a reduced price?

The Career Bridge is a new service. Founding sessions are offered at a reduced rate while I refine the service and learn from the people I work with.
